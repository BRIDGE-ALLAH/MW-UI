**Moonwitness UI is the interface language of a living knowledge ecosystem.**

It brings together design foundations, reusable interface primitives, interaction patterns, documentation, and registry tooling. Its purpose is to help different products feel coherent without forcing them into one identical experience.

## Why Moonwitness UI

Information becomes useful when people can understand its context, inspect its sources, and recognize its limits. Interfaces should make that work easier.

Moonwitness UI is guided by six principles:

- **Clarity before spectacle:** visual expression never obscures content or actions.
- **Evidence before assertion:** distinguish source material, interpretation, testimony, simulation, AI output, and verified observation.
- **Accessible by default:** semantic markup, keyboard operation, visible focus, assistive-technology feedback, responsive layouts, and reduced motion.
- **One foundation, many experiences:** share semantics and behavior while allowing each product its own composition.
- **Human responsibility:** interfaces support decisions; they do not claim authority that belongs to people or domain owners.
- **Traceable by design:** preserve attribution, provenance, rights, version, and uncertainty wherever relevant.

## An open-code foundation

This repository currently builds on React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and the shadcn/ui open-code and registry ecosystem. Components remain inspectable and customizable in the consuming project.

Moonwitness UI is the product identity and direction for this repository. It does not claim original authorship of upstream code, nor does the rebrand replace the certified `@moonwitness/moonui` implementation maintained in the MoonWitness XV ecosystem.

See the [architecture and ownership guide](https://github.com/BRIDGE-ALLAH/MW-UI/blob/main/ARCHITECTURE.md) for the current boundaries and migration principles.

## Design language

**Evidence-First Moonpunk** combines three influences:

- celestial observatory: orientation, observation, and precision;
- evidence-first editorial: readable research, archives, and source context;
- instrumental operations: clear status, timelines, and purposeful controls.

The visual language uses deep ink surfaces, warm readable foregrounds, a restrained yellow highlight, and controlled pink, cyan, and moon-purple accents. Contrast, legibility, and meaning take priority over decorative effects.

## Evidence-aware interfaces

A component must not invent a source identifier, citation, rights clearance, review approval, timestamp, confidence score, or operational status. It should render domain-owned data and clearly represent missing, unresolved, simulated, or not-yet-reviewed information.

A citation does not automatically establish truth or redistribution rights. A simulation is not a live measurement. An AI-generated value is not a substitute for accountable human review.
